What's actually used here

Most cookie policies are written to cover analytics and advertising trackers. We'd rather tell you exactly what this site does today, not what a generic template assumes it does.

In short: this site does not currently set third-party advertising or analytics cookies. It uses your browser's local storage — a similar but distinct technology — to keep you logged in and to remember that you've seen our cookie notice.

Local storage we use

KeyPurposeExpires
guest_tokenKeeps you logged into your guest account, if you have one, so you don't have to log in on every pageUntil you log out or clear browser data
guest_userRemembers basic profile info (name, id) so the site can show it without a fresh requestSame as above
cookieConsentRemembers that you've dismissed the cookie notice, so it doesn't reappear every visitUntil you clear browser data

Local storage isn't sent to us automatically with every request the way a cookie is — it's read by the page's own code to personalize what you see in your own browser. Clearing your browser's site data removes all of it.

Third-party cookies

When you pay for a booking, you're briefly interacting directly with Paystack or Stripe's own secure payment interfaces. Those providers may set their own cookies as part of fraud prevention and processing your payment — governed by their own cookie and privacy policies, not this one.

Managing your preference

You can dismiss our cookie notice using the button on it, or clear your browser's local storage for this site at any time through your browser's settings (usually under "Privacy" or "Site data"). Doing so will log you out of any guest account and bring the notice back on your next visit.
