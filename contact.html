Quick answers

Before you write.

How do I change or cancel a booking?

Open a ticket with the location below, or sign in to your guest account to manage the booking. Include your booking reference so the team can find it fast.

Is availability on the site real?

Yes. Rooms shown as available are live — the same system the front desk uses.

How quickly will I hear back?

Messages are usually answered within a few hours. Tickets send a reference to your email, and you can follow replies on this page.

I lost my ticket reference.

Check the confirmation email sent when you opened it. If it isn't there, send us a message with the email address you used.
