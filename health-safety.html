Trust & safety

Health & Safety

The standard we hold every location to — regardless of size, city, or country. If a specific branch has additional local requirements, its own page or front desk will have the details.

CCleaning & housekeeping

Every room is fully cleaned and inspected between stays before it's marked available in our system — the same real-time availability you see when booking reflects this. High-touch surfaces (door handles, light switches, remote controls) receive extra attention during turnover, and common areas follow a documented daily cleaning schedule.

TStaff training

Front desk, housekeeping, and food service staff at every branch receive hygiene and safety training as part of onboarding, refreshed periodically. This covers safe cleaning practices, food handling basics, and how to respond to a guest health or safety concern.

FFood & beverage safety

Where a branch offers dining, kitchens follow standard food safety practices — proper storage temperatures, allergen awareness, and regular equipment sanitation. If you have a food allergy or dietary restriction, let staff know when ordering; while we take this seriously, always confirm directly with kitchen staff for anything severe, since practices can vary by location.

EEmergency preparedness

Every location maintains fire safety equipment, clearly marked emergency exits, and a documented evacuation plan, in line with local building and safety regulations. First-aid supplies are available at the front desk at every branch. In a genuine emergency, always follow on-site staff instructions and local emergency services first — this page is informational, not a substitute for local emergency guidance.

AAccessibility

Accessibility features (step-free access, accessible rooms, adapted bathrooms) vary by location and building. If accessibility is important for your stay, contact the specific branch directly before booking, or reach out through our Contact page, to confirm what's available.

RReporting a concern

If something during your stay didn't meet the standard described here — cleanliness, a safety hazard, or anything else — please tell front desk staff immediately so it can be addressed while you're still there. You can also open a support ticket through our Contact page at any time, without needing an account, and it goes straight to that branch's team.

GWhat we ask of guests

  • Follow posted safety signage and staff instructions, especially during an emergency
  • Report any spill, hazard, or maintenance issue you notice, for your safety and other guests'
  • Let staff know about any accessibility or medical need that might affect your stay, so far as you're comfortable sharing
  • Respect quiet hours and shared spaces, out of consideration for other guests and staff
This page describes our general standard across locations. It doesn't replace the specific safety information posted at each branch, or the advice of local health and emergency authorities.
